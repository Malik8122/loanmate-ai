# LoanMate Bank (Demo) — General Eligibility Policy

> This is a fictional demo policy of "LoanMate Bank," created for the LoanMate AI academic project. It does not describe a real bank's policy.

## General Eligibility Principles

LoanMate evaluates a new loan application primarily using the Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR) — the proportion of a customer's monthly income already committed to existing loan EMIs plus the proposed new EMI.

## FOIR Guidance

In this demo policy:

- FOIR up to 50% of monthly income: generally eligible
- FOIR between 50% and 60%: possibly eligible, subject to further review
- FOIR above 60%: generally not eligible for the requested amount

## Credit Score

A credit score of 700 or above is generally preferred across LoanMate products. Applicants with a lower score may still be considered but are more likely to be offered a higher interest rate or a smaller loan amount.

## Section: Income Requirements by Product

| Product | Minimum monthly income (salaried) |
|---|---|
| Personal Loan | ₹25,000 |
| Home Loan | ₹30,000 |
| Vehicle Loan | ₹20,000 |

Self-employed applicants are generally assessed on annual income and business vintage instead of monthly salary.

## Section: Age and Employment

Most LoanMate products require the applicant to be between 21 and 65 years old at loan maturity, and to be salaried, self-employed, or (for home loans) an NRI, with a minimum period of employment or business operation as specified for each product.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How is loan eligibility actually calculated for a new loan?**
LoanMate's demo eligibility check estimates the EMI for the requested loan amount and tenure, adds it to any existing EMI obligations, and compares the total against the applicant's monthly income to compute FOIR, as described above.

**Is this a guaranteed approval?**
No. This is a preliminary, automated estimate for demonstration purposes only, not a final underwriting decision.
