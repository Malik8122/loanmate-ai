# LoanMate Bank (Demo) — Personal Loan

> This is a fictional demo product of "LoanMate Bank," created for the LoanMate AI academic project. It does not describe a real bank's policy.

## Overview

The LoanMate Personal Loan is an unsecured loan for personal financial needs such as medical expenses, travel, wedding costs, or home renovation. No collateral or guarantor is required.

## Interest Rate

LoanMate Personal Loans carry an interest rate of 10.5% to 16% per annum, depending on the applicant's credit score, income, and existing obligations.

## Loan Amount and Tenure

The loan amount ranges from ₹50,000 to ₹40,00,000. The repayment tenure ranges from 12 to 60 months.

## Processing Fee

A processing fee of up to 2% of the loan amount, plus applicable GST, is charged at disbursement.

## Eligibility Criteria

- Age: 21 to 60 years
- Employment: Salaried or self-employed professionals
- Minimum income (salaried): ₹25,000 per month
- Minimum income (self-employed): ₹3,00,000 per year
- Credit score: 700 and above preferred
- Work experience: Minimum 2 years, with at least 1 year in the current job (for salaried applicants)

## Required Documents — Salaried Applicants

- KYC documents (Aadhaar / PAN / Passport / Voter ID)
- Last 3 months' salary slips
- Last 6 months' bank statements
- Form 16 or IT returns for the last 2 years
- Employment certificate or offer letter

## Required Documents — Self-Employed Applicants

- KYC documents (Aadhaar / PAN)
- Last 2 years' IT returns with computation
- Last 6 months' bank statements
- Business proof (GST registration / trade licence)
- Certified financials (profit & loss statement, balance sheet)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I get a personal loan without collateral?**
Yes. LoanMate Personal Loans are unsecured — no collateral or guarantor is required.

**How quickly is the loan disbursed?**
For eligible applicants with complete documentation, disbursal can happen within 24–48 hours.

**Can I prepay my personal loan?**
Yes. Prepayment is allowed after 12 EMIs. See the LoanMate Prepayment Policy for charges that may apply.
