# RBI Charter of Customer Rights (Public Information)

> Public information: a plain-language summary of the RBI Charter of Customer Rights (released 3 December 2014). Banks were asked to adopt a Board-approved customer rights policy based on it. General information — check rbi.org.in for the current text. Last reviewed: October 2026.

## The Five Rights

The Charter sets out five basic rights of every bank customer, covering the time before a product is taken, while it is in use, and after it ends. Each bank turns them into its own customer rights policy.

## 1. Right to Fair Treatment

Customers must be treated with courtesy and must not be discriminated against on grounds such as gender, age, religion, caste or physical ability. Banks should make their services accessible to everyone.

## 2. Right to Transparency, Fair and Honest Dealing

Banks must give clear, simple information about a product's terms, costs, rights and obligations, and must not use misleading or unfair practices. For loans, the Key Fact Statement and the disclosure of every charge put this right into practice.

## 3. Right to Suitability

The products offered should suit the customer's needs and financial situation, assessed from information the customer provides. A bank should not push products a customer does not need.

## 4. Right to Privacy

Customers' personal information must be kept confidential and used only for the purpose it was collected for, unless the customer agrees or the law requires otherwise. Banks must not use customer data for unsolicited marketing without consent.

## 5. Right to Grievance Redress and Compensation

Customers have the right to complain and get a response, and to be compensated where the bank is at fault, as per the bank's policy and RBI rules. If the bank does not resolve a complaint, the customer can escalate it to the RBI Ombudsman.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What rights do I have as a bank customer?**
Under the RBI Charter of Customer Rights: fair treatment; transparency and honest dealing; products suited to your needs; privacy of your information; and grievance redress with compensation where the bank is at fault.
