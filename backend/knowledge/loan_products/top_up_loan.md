# LoanMate Bank (Demo) — Top-up Loan

> This is a fictional demo product of "LoanMate Bank," created for the LoanMate AI academic project. It does not describe a real bank's policy. Its design follows how top-up loans commonly work at Indian banks.

## Overview

A LoanMate Top-up Loan gives existing borrowers additional funds on top of their current home, car or personal loan, without a fresh application from scratch. The top-up runs alongside the existing loan with its own EMI. It can be used for any genuine personal need — home renovation, education, medical expenses, a wedding, or consolidating costlier debt — but not for speculative purposes such as trading in shares.

## Who Can Get a Top-up

Existing LoanMate borrowers whose loan is active and has a clean repayment record: at least 12 EMIs paid on time for a home loan, or at least 6 EMIs paid on time for a car or personal loan, with no EMI currently overdue. Final eligibility depends on the customer's income, credit score and, for home loans, the current property value. Being offered a top-up on a call is not an approval.

## Interest Rate and Amount

In this demo policy, a home-loan top-up is priced 0.25% to 1% above the customer's existing home loan rate, and a car or personal loan top-up at the bank's prevailing personal loan rate of 10.5% to 16% per annum. For a home loan, the existing loan plus the top-up cannot exceed the RBI's loan-to-value limits on the property's current value. Personal and car loan top-ups range from ₹50,000 to ₹10 lakh.

## Tenure and Charges

A home-loan top-up can run up to the remaining tenure of the home loan, to a maximum of 15 years. Car and personal loan top-ups run up to 5 years. A processing fee of up to 1% of the top-up amount plus GST applies. Floating-rate top-ups taken by individuals carry no prepayment charges, as per RBI rules.

## Documents

Usually only updated KYC, the latest 3 months' salary slips or latest income proof, and the last 6 months' bank statements. Property documents are already held for the existing home loan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is a top-up cheaper than a personal loan?**
A home-loan top-up is usually cheaper, because it is secured by the same property. A car or personal loan top-up is priced like a personal loan.

**Is the interest on a home-loan top-up tax deductible?**
Only if the money is used to buy, build or renovate a house, with proof. Otherwise there is no tax benefit.
