# RBI Rules on Prepayment and Foreclosure Charges (Public Information)

> Public information: a plain-language summary of Reserve Bank of India (RBI) rules that apply to all banks in India. Sources: RBI circular on foreclosure charges on floating-rate term loans (May 2012) and the RBI (Pre-payment Charges on Loans) Directions, 2025 (issued 2 July 2025). General information, not legal advice — check rbi.org.in for the current text. Last reviewed: October 2026.

## Floating-Rate Loans to Individuals — No Prepayment Charges

Since 2012, banks in India are not allowed to charge any foreclosure charge or prepayment penalty on floating-rate term loans given to individual borrowers. This covers most floating-rate home loans. A borrower can prepay part of the loan or close it fully at any time without a penalty.

## RBI Pre-payment Charges Directions, 2025

For loans sanctioned or renewed on or after 1 January 2026, the RBI's 2025 directions say:

- No prepayment charges on any floating-rate loan given to an individual for a non-business purpose, with or without co-borrowers.
- No prepayment charges on floating-rate loans for business purposes given to individuals and micro and small enterprises (MSEs) by commercial banks (other than small finance banks, regional rural banks and local area banks), Tier 4 urban co-operative banks, upper-layer NBFCs and all-India financial institutions.
- Small finance banks, regional rural banks, Tier 3 urban co-operative banks, state and central co-operative banks and middle-layer NBFCs cannot charge prepayment charges on such business loans up to ₹50 lakh.
- The rule applies whatever the source of the money used to prepay, and no minimum lock-in period can be imposed.

## Fixed-Rate and Other Loans

Prepayment or foreclosure charges may still apply to fixed-rate loans and loans outside the categories above. Any such charge must be disclosed upfront in the loan's Key Fact Statement (KFS) and sanction letter; a lender cannot levy a charge that was not disclosed. For hybrid (fixed-then-floating) loans, the no-charge rule applies if the loan is on a floating rate when it is prepaid.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can my bank charge me for closing my floating-rate home loan early?**
No. For individual borrowers, floating-rate loans cannot carry foreclosure or prepayment charges under RBI rules.

**I took a fixed-rate personal loan. Can there be a prepayment charge?**
Yes, fixed-rate loans can carry a prepayment charge, but only at the rate disclosed in your Key Fact Statement and loan agreement.

**Does it matter whether I prepay with my own savings or a loan from another bank (balance transfer)?**
No. Under the 2025 directions, the no-charge rule applies regardless of the source of funds.
