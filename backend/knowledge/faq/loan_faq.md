# LoanMate Bank (Demo) — General Loan FAQ

> This is fictional demo FAQ content for "LoanMate Bank," created for the LoanMate AI academic project.

## Section: Applying

**How do I apply for a loan?**
You can tell LoanMate the type of loan and amount you're interested in during a conversation, and LoanMate will collect initial details. A loan specialist follows up for full processing — this demo does not complete a real loan application.

**How do I check my loan eligibility?**
You can ask LoanMate for a preliminary eligibility estimate based on your income and the loan amount you're considering, or visit a branch for a detailed assessment.

## Section: Products

**What loan products does LoanMate offer?**
LoanMate offers Personal Loans, Home Loans, Vehicle Loans, Education Loans, and Business Loans. Ask about any one of these for full details on rates, eligibility, and documents.

**Which loan type has the lowest interest rate?**
Among LoanMate's demo products, Home Loans typically carry the lowest interest rate range (8.25%–10.5% per annum), reflecting that they are secured against property.

## Section: Credit Score

**What credit score do I need?**
A score of 700 or above is generally preferred across LoanMate products. A higher score can also improve the interest rate offered.

## Section: Collateral

**Which LoanMate loans require collateral?**
Personal Loans are unsecured (no collateral). Home Loans are secured by the property being financed. Vehicle Loans are secured by the vehicle. Education Loans above ₹7.5 lakhs generally require collateral. Business Loans up to ₹50 lakhs may be unsecured for eligible businesses.
