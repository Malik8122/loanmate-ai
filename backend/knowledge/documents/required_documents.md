# LoanMate Bank (Demo) — Required Documents by Loan Type

> This is fictional demo content for "LoanMate Bank," created for the LoanMate AI academic project.

## Section: Personal Loan Documents

Salaried applicants: KYC documents (Aadhaar / PAN / Passport / Voter ID), last 3 months' salary slips, last 6 months' bank statements, Form 16 or IT returns for the last 2 years, and an employment certificate or offer letter.

Self-employed applicants: KYC documents (Aadhaar / PAN), last 2 years' IT returns with computation, last 6 months' bank statements, business proof (GST registration or trade licence), and certified financials (profit & loss statement, balance sheet).

## Section: Home Loan Documents

KYC documents (Aadhaar / PAN / Passport), last 3 months' salary slips (salaried applicants), last 2 years' IT returns, last 6 months' bank statements, property documents (sale deed, allotment letter, or approved building plan), and a no-objection certificate (NOC) from the builder or housing society.

## Section: Vehicle Loan Documents

KYC documents (Aadhaar / PAN / Driving Licence), last 3 months' salary slips or 2 years' IT returns, last 6 months' bank statements, vehicle proforma invoice (or registration certificate for used vehicles), and insurance documents.

## Section: Education Loan Documents

KYC documents of both the student and the co-applicant, admission letter from the institution, fee structure or fee schedule, academic records (mark sheets, certificates), income proof of the co-applicant, and collateral documents for loans above ₹7.5 lakhs.

## Section: Business Loan Documents

KYC of the proprietor, directors, or partners; business proof (GST registration, incorporation certificate, or trade licence); last 2 years' IT returns with computation; last 12 months' bank statements; certified financials (audited profit & loss statement, balance sheet); and the last 12 months' GST returns.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to submit documents in person?**
This demo does not process real document submission. In a real deployment, documents would typically be uploaded through a secure portal or submitted at a branch.

**What if I'm missing one document?**
LoanMate's demo policy allows conditional processing with most required documents, with the missing item to be submitted before final disbursal — this varies by loan type and amount.
