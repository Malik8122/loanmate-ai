# RBI Rules for Digital Loans and Loan Apps (Public Information)

> Public information: a plain-language summary of the RBI guidelines on digital lending (September 2022), consolidated into the RBI (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025. Applies to all banks and regulated lenders in India. General information, not legal advice. Last reviewed: October 2026.

## Money Flows Only Between You and the Lender

For any loan taken through an app or website, the loan must be paid directly into the borrower's own bank account, and repayments must go directly into the regulated lender's account. A loan app or agent (Lending Service Provider) cannot route the money through its own or a third party's account. Any fees payable to the app must be paid by the lender, not charged directly to the borrower.

## Key Fact Statement and Cooling-Off Period

The borrower must get a Key Fact Statement with the all-in Annual Percentage Rate (APR) before signing. Digital loans must also offer a cooling-off (look-up) period — at least three days for loans with a tenure of seven days or more, and one day for shorter loans — during which the borrower can exit by repaying the principal and proportionate APR, without any penalty.

## Data and Phone Access

Lending apps may collect only the data needed for the loan, with the borrower's explicit consent. They must not access the phone's contact list, photos, media files or call logs (one-time access for KYC to camera, microphone and location is allowed with consent).

## Check the App Is Genuine

Every regulated lender must publish the list of digital lending apps and partners it works with on its website, and the RBI maintains a public directory of digital lending apps of regulated entities on its website. Loan apps not linked to a bank or RBI-registered NBFC are illegal and are a common source of harassment and fraud.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**A loan app is threatening to message my contacts. What should I do?**
Genuine regulated lenders cannot access your contacts or harass you. Report the app at cybercrime.gov.in or by calling 1930, and complain to the lender and the RBI Ombudsman if the lender is regulated.
