# LoanMate Bank (Demo) — General Banking FAQ

> This is fictional demo FAQ content for "LoanMate Bank," created for the LoanMate AI academic project.

## Section: About LoanMate

**What is LoanMate?**
LoanMate is a demo conversational voice assistant that helps customers of a fictional bank get information about their loans and ask general loan-related questions, in English, Hindi, or Hinglish.

## Section: Talking to a Human

**Can I speak to a real person?**
Yes. If you ask to speak with a human agent, a loan officer, or customer care, LoanMate will acknowledge the request and let you know it will be flagged for follow-up. In this demo, no real human agent is actually connected.

## Section: Calling LoanMate vs LoanMate Calling You

**What can I do when I call LoanMate?**
When you call, LoanMate is your service desk: ask about your EMI, balance, due date or loan status, ask about products and policies, or request a service — an account statement, a no-dues certificate, a foreclosure quote, an interest certificate, a complaint, or a callback from a human officer. Every request gets a reference number.

**Why would LoanMate call me?**
LoanMate calls only for a specific reason: a reminder about an upcoming EMI, a follow-up on an overdue payment, a pending KYC update, or a product update such as a top-up loan offer. It will first confirm it is speaking to the right person before sharing any account details, and it will respect a request to call back later or not to call again.

**Will LoanMate ask for my OTP or PIN?**
Never. Neither LoanMate nor any genuine bank asks for an OTP, PIN, CVV or password on a call.

## Section: Complaints

**How do I complain about a service issue?**
Tell LoanMate on a call and it will register a complaint with a reference number. If a complaint is not resolved within 30 days, or you are not satisfied with the reply, you can escalate it to the RBI Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in or on the toll-free number 14448.

## Section: Deposit Insurance

**Are bank deposits insured?**
Yes. Deposits in banks in India are insured by the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC), an RBI subsidiary, up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank, covering principal and interest together.

## Section: Data and Privacy

**Is my information safe with LoanMate?**
This is a demonstration project using mock, non-real customer and loan data. It does not represent the security practices of a real bank and should not be evaluated as production banking software.

## Section: Language Support

**What languages does LoanMate support?**
LoanMate understands and responds in English, Hindi, and Hinglish (a natural mix of Hindi and English). Other languages, including Urdu, are not supported.
