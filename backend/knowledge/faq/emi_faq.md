# LoanMate Bank (Demo) — EMI FAQ

> This is fictional demo FAQ content for "LoanMate Bank," created for the LoanMate AI academic project. For a specific customer's actual EMI amount, outstanding balance, or due date, LoanMate looks up the real value from the customer's account rather than relying on this general document.

## Section: What Is an EMI

An EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed monthly payment a borrower makes towards a loan, covering both a portion of the principal and the interest for that month, calculated using a standard reducing-balance formula.

## Section: Payment Methods

**How can I make my EMI payment?**
EMIs are collected via ECS (auto-debit) from the customer's registered bank account on the due date. Payments can also be made through net banking, UPI, or at a branch.

## Section: Changing Your EMI

**Can I change my EMI due date?**
LoanMate's demo policy allows a one-time change of EMI due date per loan, subject to the new date being within the same billing cycle window. This would need to be requested through a branch or the customer support team, not through this voice assistant.

**Can I reduce my EMI amount?**
An EMI amount can be reduced either by making a prepayment (see the Prepayment Policy) or by extending the loan's remaining tenure, which increases total interest paid over the life of the loan.

## Section: Understanding Your EMI Breakdown

Each EMI is split into a principal component and an interest component. In the early part of a loan's tenure, a larger share of each EMI goes towards interest; as the loan matures, a larger share goes towards principal. This is standard for reducing-balance loans and is not specific to any one LoanMate product.
