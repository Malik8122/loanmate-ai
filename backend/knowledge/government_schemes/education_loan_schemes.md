# Government Education Loan Schemes (Public Information)

> Public information: a plain-language summary of the Indian Banks' Association (IBA) Model Education Loan Scheme and Ministry of Education schemes — PM-Vidyalaxmi (approved November 2024), Central Sector Interest Subsidy (CSIS) and the Credit Guarantee Fund Scheme for Education Loans (CGFSEL). General information — check pmvidyalaxmi.co.in and the lender for current terms. Last reviewed: October 2026.

## IBA Model Education Loan Scheme

Most banks follow the IBA model scheme for education loans in India and abroad. The repayment holiday (moratorium) is the course period plus one year. Repayment can then be spread over up to 15 years. For loans up to ₹4 lakh no margin money is usually needed, and loans up to ₹7.5 lakh are generally given without collateral, backed by a government credit guarantee.

## PM-Vidyalaxmi

PM-Vidyalaxmi offers collateral-free and guarantor-free education loans to students admitted to quality higher education institutions (based on national NIRF rankings). For loans up to ₹7.5 lakh, the Government guarantees 75% of any default to the bank. Students with family income up to ₹8 lakh a year, not covered by other schemes, get 3% interest subvention during the moratorium on loans up to ₹10 lakh. Applications are made on the PM-Vidyalaxmi portal.

## Central Sector Interest Subsidy (CSIS)

Students from families with annual income up to ₹4.5 lakh can get full interest subsidy during the moratorium period on education loans up to ₹10 lakh taken under the IBA model scheme for recognised courses in India. This is in addition to the PM-Vidyalaxmi 3% subvention for the ₹4.5–8 lakh income band.

## Tax Benefit on Education Loan Interest

Under the old income-tax regime, the full interest paid on an education loan for higher studies of yourself, your spouse or children can be deducted (Section 80E of the Income-tax Act, 1961), with no upper limit, for up to 8 years from the year repayment starts. See the loan tax benefits document for details.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need collateral for a ₹6 lakh education loan?**
Usually not — loans up to ₹7.5 lakh are generally collateral-free under the IBA model scheme and the government credit guarantee, though the bank may still ask for a parent or guardian as co-borrower.
