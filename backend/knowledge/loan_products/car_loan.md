# LoanMate Bank (Demo) — Car Loan

> This is a fictional demo product of "LoanMate Bank," created for the LoanMate AI academic project. It does not describe a real bank's policy. Figures are in line with typical ranges at Indian banks.

## Overview

The LoanMate Car Loan finances the purchase of a new car, or a used car up to 5 years old. The car is hypothecated to the bank — the bank's interest is recorded on the registration certificate (RC) until the loan is fully repaid.

## Interest Rate

New car loans carry an interest rate of 8.75% to 12% per annum, and used car loans 11% to 15% per annum, depending on the customer's credit score and income. Car loans are usually on a fixed rate.

## Loan Amount and Tenure

Up to 90% of the on-road price for new cars and up to 80% of the valuation for used cars. The repayment tenure is 1 to 7 years for new cars and up to 5 years for used cars.

## Charges

A processing fee of up to 1% of the loan amount plus GST. Fixed-rate car loans carry a prepayment or foreclosure charge of up to 5% of the outstanding principal if closed within the first 12 months and up to 3% after that, as stated in the Key Fact Statement.

## Eligibility and Documents

Age 21 to 65 years, minimum income of ₹25,000 per month (salaried) or ₹3 lakh per year (self-employed), and a credit score of 700 or above preferred. Documents: KYC, the latest 3 months' salary slips or 2 years' IT returns, the last 6 months' bank statements, and the car's pro-forma invoice.

## After Closing the Loan

Once the loan is repaid, LoanMate issues a no-objection certificate (NOC) and Form 35 so the hypothecation can be removed from the RC at the RTO. Under RBI rules, original documents must be returned within 30 days of closure.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I sell my car before the loan is closed?**
Only after closing the loan or with the bank's consent, because the car is hypothecated to the bank.
