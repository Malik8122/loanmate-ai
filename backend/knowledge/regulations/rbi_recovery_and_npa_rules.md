# Overdue Loans, NPA Classification and Fair Recovery Practices (Public Information)

> Public information: a plain-language summary of RBI rules on asset classification (Prudential norms, including the clarification of 12 November 2021) and on conduct of recovery agents (circular of 12 August 2022). Applies to all banks in India. General information, not legal advice. Last reviewed: October 2026.

## Special Mention Accounts (SMA)

Banks flag a loan as soon as an EMI is overdue, in stages: SMA-0 when it is overdue for up to 30 days, SMA-1 for 31 to 60 days, and SMA-2 for 61 to 90 days. These stages are reported to credit bureaus and can lower the borrower's credit score.

## Non-Performing Asset (NPA)

A loan becomes a Non-Performing Asset (NPA) when principal or interest stays overdue for more than 90 days. An NPA account is upgraded back to "standard" only when the entire overdue amount — all arrears of interest and principal — is paid; paying just one EMI is not enough.

## Fair Recovery Practices

Banks and their recovery agents must not use intimidation or harassment of any kind, verbal or physical. They must not call borrowers before 8 a.m. or after 7 p.m. for recovery. They must not publicly humiliate the borrower, intrude on the privacy of family members, friends or colleagues, send inappropriate messages, or make threatening or anonymous calls. A bank is responsible for the conduct of the recovery agents it hires.

## Restructuring and Talking to the Bank Early

Borrowers facing genuine financial difficulty should contact their bank before missing EMIs. Banks can, as per their board policy, consider options such as rescheduling EMIs, extending the tenure, or restructuring the loan. A restructured loan may be reported as such to credit bureaus.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**A recovery agent called me at 10 p.m. and threatened me. What can I do?**
That breaks RBI rules. Note the date, time and number, complain to the bank in writing, and if the bank does not resolve it within 30 days, complain to the RBI Ombudsman. Threats can also be reported to the police.

**After how many missed EMIs does a loan become an NPA?**
When any amount stays overdue for more than 90 days — roughly three missed EMIs in a row.
