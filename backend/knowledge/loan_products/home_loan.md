# LoanMate Bank (Demo) — Home Loan

> This is a fictional demo product of "LoanMate Bank," created for the LoanMate AI academic project. It does not describe a real bank's policy.

## Overview

The LoanMate Home Loan finances the purchase, construction, or renovation of a residential property. Both floating and fixed interest rate options are available.

## Interest Rate

LoanMate Home Loans carry an interest rate of 8.25% to 10.5% per annum.

## Loan Amount and Tenure

The loan amount ranges from ₹5,00,000 to ₹10,00,00,000. The repayment tenure ranges from 5 to 30 years.

## Processing Fee

A processing fee of 0.5% to 1% of the loan amount, plus applicable GST, is charged at disbursement.

## Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio

LoanMate finances up to 90% of the property value for loans up to ₹30 lakhs, and up to 80% of the property value for higher loan amounts.

## Eligibility Criteria

- Age: 21 to 65 years (at loan maturity)
- Employment: Salaried, self-employed, or NRI applicants
- Minimum income (salaried): ₹30,000 per month
- Credit score: 675 and above
- Property must be in an approved location or a RERA-registered project

## Required Documents

- KYC documents (Aadhaar / PAN / Passport)
- Last 3 months' salary slips (salaried applicants)
- Last 2 years' IT returns
- Last 6 months' bank statements
- Property documents (sale deed, allotment letter, or approved building plan)
- No-objection certificate (NOC) from the builder or housing society

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the maximum LTV ratio?**
Up to 90% of property value for loans up to ₹30 lakhs, and up to 75–80% for higher loan amounts.

**Can I apply jointly for a home loan?**
Yes. Joint applications with a co-applicant, such as a spouse or parent, are allowed and may improve loan eligibility.

**Is there a tax benefit on home loans?**
Yes, under Indian income tax law generally: principal repayment (Section 80C, up to ₹1.5 lakh) and interest paid (Section 24(b), up to ₹2 lakh). This is general information, not tax advice — please consult a tax professional for your specific situation.
