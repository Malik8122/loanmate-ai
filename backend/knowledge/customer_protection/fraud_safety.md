# Banking Fraud Safety and Genuine Bank Calls (Public Information)

> Public information: a plain-language summary of RBI guidance on customer protection in unauthorised electronic transactions (circular of 6 July 2017), the RBI circular on use of 1600xx and 140xx numbers for bank calls (17 January 2025), and Government of India cyber-fraud reporting channels. General information, not legal advice. Last reviewed: October 2026.

## What a Bank Will Never Ask For

A genuine bank, its staff, or its voice assistant will never ask for an OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, CVV, net-banking password, or full card number — not on a call, SMS, email, WhatsApp or link. A genuine bank will also never ask a customer to install a screen-sharing or remote-access app, or to "verify KYC" by clicking a link.

## How to Recognise a Genuine Bank Call

Under RBI's January 2025 instructions, banks are to use numbers starting with 1600 for service and transaction-related calls, and numbers starting with 140 for promotional calls. Calls about your account from ordinary 10-digit mobile numbers should be treated with suspicion. When in doubt, hang up and call the number printed on the back of your card or on the bank's official website.

## If You Are a Victim of Fraud

Act immediately: call the national cyber fraud helpline 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in, and inform your bank so it can block the card or account. Suspected fraud calls and messages, even if no money was lost, can be reported through Chakshu on the Sanchar Saathi portal.

## Your Liability for Unauthorised Transactions

Under RBI rules, if an unauthorised electronic transaction happened because of a fault at the bank's end, or a third-party breach that the customer reports within 3 working days, the customer has zero liability. If reported within 4 to 7 working days, the customer's liability is limited to the transaction amount or a cap of ₹5,000 to ₹25,000 depending on the account type, whichever is lower. The bank must credit the disputed amount (shadow reversal) within 10 working days of being informed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Someone from "the bank" said my loan EMI bounced and I must pay through a link. Is it real?**
Treat it as suspicious. Pay only through your bank's official app, website, branch or auto-debit — never through a link sent by an unknown caller.

**I shared my OTP by mistake. What now?**
Call your bank right away to block the card or account, then call 1930 and report at cybercrime.gov.in. Reporting within 3 working days protects your rights under RBI rules.
