# LoanMate Bank (Demo) — General Banking FAQ

> This is fictional demo FAQ content for "LoanMate Bank," created for the LoanMate AI academic project.

## Section: About LoanMate

**What is LoanMate?**
LoanMate is a demo conversational voice assistant that helps customers of a fictional bank get information about their loans and ask general loan-related questions, in English, Hindi, or Hinglish.

## Section: Talking to a Human

**Can I speak to a real person?**
Yes. If you ask to speak with a human agent, a loan officer, or customer care, LoanMate will acknowledge the request and let you know it will be flagged for follow-up. In this demo, no real human agent is actually connected.

## Section: Data and Privacy

**Is my information safe with LoanMate?**
This is a demonstration project using mock, non-real customer and loan data. It does not represent the security practices of a real bank and should not be evaluated as production banking software.

## Section: Language Support

**What languages does LoanMate support?**
LoanMate understands and responds in English, Hindi, and Hinglish (a natural mix of Hindi and English). Other languages, including Urdu, are not supported.
