# Home Loan Balance Transfer (Public Information)

> Public information: a plain-language explanation of how a home loan balance transfer works at Indian banks, read together with the RBI rules on prepayment charges and return of property documents. General information, not financial advice — each lender sets its own fees and process. Last reviewed: October 2026.

## What a Balance Transfer Is

A balance transfer moves an existing home loan to another lender, usually to get a lower interest rate. The new lender pays off the old loan in full, takes over the property as security, and the borrower then pays EMIs to the new lender. Many lenders also offer a top-up loan along with the transfer.

## When It Makes Sense

A transfer helps most when the rate difference is meaningful and many years of tenure remain, because most interest is paid in the early years. Compare the new lender's Annual Percentage Rate (APR) from its Key Fact Statement, not just the interest rate, because the transfer has its own costs. Before switching, borrowers can also ask their current lender to reduce the rate or move an old MCLR-linked loan to a repo-linked rate, which is often cheaper than moving.

## Steps

1. Apply to the new lender and get a sanction letter.
2. Ask the current lender for a foreclosure letter showing the exact payoff amount, and a list of the original property documents it holds.
3. The new lender pays the payoff amount directly to the old lender.
4. The old lender closes the loan, releases the original property documents within 30 days (as required by RBI) and removes its charge on the property.
5. The documents are deposited with the new lender, which registers its own charge.

## Costs

There is no prepayment or foreclosure charge for moving a floating-rate home loan taken by an individual — RBI rules prohibit it, whatever the source of the money. The new lender may charge a processing fee, legal and valuation fees, and stamp duty on the new mortgage, which varies by state. A fixed-rate loan may carry a foreclosure charge, as stated in its loan agreement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will my current bank charge me for transferring my home loan?**
Not for a floating-rate home loan taken as an individual — RBI rules bar prepayment charges on those. Fixed-rate loans may carry a charge stated in your agreement.

**How long does a balance transfer take?**
Usually a few weeks, depending on both lenders' paperwork. The old lender must return your property documents within 30 days of closure.
