# Complaints, Grievance Redressal and the RBI Ombudsman (Public Information)

> Public information: a plain-language summary of the Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026 (effective 1 July 2026, replacing the 2021 scheme). Applies to all banks and RBI-regulated entities. General information, not legal advice — check rbi.org.in and cms.rbi.org.in for the current text. Last reviewed: October 2026.

## Step 1 — Complain to the Bank First

A complaint must first be made to the bank itself — at a branch, through its customer care, website or app, or to its grievance redressal or nodal officer, whose contact details every bank must publish. The bank should acknowledge the complaint and give a reference number. Banks generally have up to 30 days to resolve a complaint.

## Step 2 — RBI Ombudsman

If the bank rejects the complaint, does not reply within the allowed time, or the customer is not satisfied with the reply, the customer can complain to the RBI Ombudsman. Under the 2026 scheme the complaint must be filed within 90 days from the end of the bank's response period or from the bank's last reply, whichever is later. Filing is free and does not need a lawyer.

## How to File

Online at the RBI Complaint Management System (cms.rbi.org.in), by email, or by post to the RBI's Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre. The RBI's toll-free contact centre number is 14448, which also helps with filing and tracking complaints.

## Compensation

The Ombudsman can award compensation of up to ₹30 lakh for the actual financial loss suffered, plus up to ₹3 lakh for the customer's loss of time, expenses, harassment and mental anguish. There is no limit on the amount in dispute that can be complained about.

## What the Ombudsman Does Not Handle

Commercial decisions of the bank (such as whether to approve a loan or the interest rate it offers, unless a regulation is broken), matters already before a court or another forum, and disputes about services outside the RBI's purview. Either side can appeal an Ombudsman's decision to the Appellate Authority within 30 days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**My bank hasn't returned my property papers two months after I closed my loan. Can I complain?**
Yes. Complain to the bank first; if it is not resolved, the RBI Ombudsman can take it up. RBI rules also require the bank to pay ₹5,000 per day of delay beyond 30 days.
