# Return of Property Documents After Loan Closure (Public Information)

> Public information: a plain-language summary of the RBI circular "Responsible Lending Conduct – Release of Movable / Immovable Property Documents on Repayment / Settlement of Personal Loans" (13 September 2023, effective 1 December 2023). Applies to all banks and regulated lenders in India. General information, not legal advice. Last reviewed: October 2026.

## 30-Day Deadline

After a borrower fully repays or settles a loan, the lender must return all original movable and immovable property documents (for example, the sale deed of a house or the registration papers of a vehicle) and remove any charge registered with a registry, within 30 days.

## Where to Collect

The borrower can choose to collect the original documents either from the branch where the loan account was serviced or from any other office of the lender where the documents are kept. The place and timeline for returning documents must be stated in the sanction letter.

## Compensation for Delay

If the lender delays returning the documents or removing the charge beyond 30 days for reasons attributable to the lender, it must tell the borrower the reasons and pay compensation of ₹5,000 for each day of delay.

## Lost or Damaged Documents

If original documents are lost or damaged while with the lender, the lender must help the borrower get duplicate or certified copies and bear all the costs, in addition to the compensation. The lender gets an extra 30 days for this (60 days in total); the ₹5,000-per-day compensation applies after that.

## Death of the Borrower

Lenders must have a clear, published procedure for returning original documents to the legal heirs if the sole or joint borrower dies.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**I closed my home loan. What should I collect from the bank?**
The original property documents, a no-dues or loan closure certificate, and confirmation that the bank's charge on the property has been removed (for example, from CERSAI).
