# LoanMate Bank (Demo) — Missed EMI Policy

> This is a fictional demo policy of "LoanMate Bank," created for the LoanMate AI academic project. It does not describe a real bank's policy.

## What Happens If You Miss an EMI

If an EMI payment is not received by its due date, LoanMate's demo policy is as follows:

1. A penal charge is levied (see the Charges section below).
2. The missed payment may be reported to credit bureaus, which can negatively affect the customer's credit score.
3. LoanMate will attempt to contact the customer to understand the reason for the missed payment and discuss options.

## Section: Charges

A penal charge of 2% of the missed EMI amount, or ₹500, whichever is higher, applies per missed EMI in this demo policy. As required by RBI rules, this is a fixed penal charge — it is not added to the loan's interest rate, and no interest is charged on it. If an auto-debit (NACH) mandate fails for lack of funds, a bounce charge of ₹500 plus GST also applies.

## Grace Period

LoanMate's demo policy allows a grace period of 3 days from the due date before a payment is formally marked as "missed" and the late fee is applied. Payments made within this grace period are not treated as late.

## Section: Repeated Missed Payments

Any overdue EMI is reported to credit bureaus: under RBI rules the loan is flagged SMA-0 when overdue up to 30 days, SMA-1 at 31–60 days and SMA-2 at 61–90 days, and becomes a Non-Performing Asset (NPA) once overdue for more than 90 days. LoanMate may then initiate a formal recovery process, always following RBI's fair recovery rules — no harassment, and no recovery calls before 8 a.m. or after 7 p.m. Customers experiencing financial difficulty are strongly encouraged to contact LoanMate proactively before missing a payment, as restructuring or a revised repayment plan may be possible.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What should I do if I know I will miss an upcoming EMI?**
Contact LoanMate as early as possible. Proactively flagging a payment difficulty gives more options for resolution than a payment that is simply missed without notice.

**Does a single missed EMI close my loan?**
No. A single missed EMI results in a late fee and a potential credit score impact, but does not close the loan. Loan status changes to "Overdue" when payments remain unpaid, and to NPA after more than 90 days overdue, as described above.
