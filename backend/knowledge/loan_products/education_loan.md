# LoanMate Bank (Demo) — Education Loan

> This is a fictional demo product of "LoanMate Bank," created for the LoanMate AI academic project. It does not describe a real bank's policy.

## Overview

The LoanMate Education Loan funds higher education in India or abroad, covering tuition, living expenses, and related academic costs.

## Interest Rate

LoanMate Education Loans carry an interest rate of 9% to 13% per annum.

## Loan Amount and Tenure

Up to ₹1,50,00,000 for study in India, and up to ₹2,00,00,000 for study abroad. The repayment tenure is up to 15 years.

## Moratorium Period

Repayment is not required during the course duration plus an additional 12 months after course completion. This is known as the moratorium period.

## Processing Fee

No processing fee for loans up to ₹10 lakhs. A 1% processing fee applies above that amount.

## Eligibility Criteria

- Applicant must be a student admitted to a recognised institution
- A parent or guardian is required as a co-applicant
- Covers graduate, post-graduate, professional, and vocational courses

## Required Documents

- KYC documents of both the student and the co-applicant
- Admission letter from the institution
- Fee structure or fee schedule
- Academic records (mark sheets, certificates)
- Income proof of the co-applicant
- Collateral documents, required for loans above ₹7.5 lakhs

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When does repayment start?**
Repayment begins after the moratorium period ends — that is, course completion plus 12 months, or 6 months after the student starts a job, whichever comes first.

**Is there a tax benefit?**
Interest paid on education loans is generally deductible under Section 80E of the Indian Income Tax Act for up to 8 years. This is general information, not tax advice.
