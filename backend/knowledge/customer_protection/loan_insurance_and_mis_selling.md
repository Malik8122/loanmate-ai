# Insurance With Loans and Mis-Selling (Public Information)

> Public information: a plain-language summary of RBI rules on disclosure of charges in the Key Fact Statement (April 2024), the RBI directions on preventing mis-selling and compulsory bundling of products (issued June 2026, effective 1 January 2027), and the IRDAI free-look period for life insurance (Master Circular, June 2024). General information, not legal advice — check rbi.org.in and irdai.gov.in for the current text. Last reviewed: October 2026.

## Loan Protection Insurance

Lenders often offer insurance with a loan, such as a credit life or term policy that pays off the loan if the borrower dies or is disabled. It can be useful, especially for a large home loan, but it is a separate product with its own premium. The borrower can choose the insurer and the cover, and can use a policy they already hold where the lender accepts it.

## Insurance Is Not a Condition of the Loan

From 1 January 2027, RBI's mis-selling directions prohibit banks from "compulsory bundling" — making a loan or any banking service conditional on buying insurance, investment or any other product. Where insurance genuinely protects the loan, the customer remains free to buy it from any insurer. Consent to any extra product must be explicit and specific to that product. Even before 2027, any insurance premium financed or collected with a loan must appear in the Key Fact Statement, and a charge not disclosed there cannot be levied without the borrower's consent.

## If a Product Was Mis-Sold

Mis-selling means selling a product the customer did not need, did not understand, or was told was compulsory. Under the 2026 directions, where mis-selling is established, the bank must refund the amount paid for that product. Complain to the bank first; if it is not resolved, escalate to the RBI Ombudsman.

## Free-Look Period

A newly bought life insurance policy has a 30-day free-look period from receipt of the policy document, during which the policyholder can return it if the terms don't suit them and get a refund, minus small deductions allowed by IRDAI.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**The bank says I must buy their insurance to get my home loan. Is that true?**
No. Insurance can be recommended, but from 1 January 2027 RBI prohibits making a loan conditional on buying it, and you are free to choose any insurer for genuine loan cover. Any premium must be shown in your Key Fact Statement.

**I was sold insurance with my loan without understanding it. What can I do?**
If you received the policy recently, you may be within the 30-day free-look period and can return it. Otherwise, complain to the bank and, if needed, the RBI Ombudsman; where mis-selling is established, the bank must refund what you paid.
