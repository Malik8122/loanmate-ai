# PMAY-Urban 2.0 Home Loan Interest Subsidy (Public Information)

> Public information: a plain-language summary of the Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS) under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Urban 2.0, run by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government of India. Source: pmaymis.gov.in. General information — eligibility is decided by the lender and the scheme's nodal agencies. Last reviewed: October 2026.

## What the Scheme Offers

PMAY-U 2.0's Interest Subsidy Scheme gives first-time home buyers in urban areas from lower- and middle-income families a government subsidy on home loan interest. It applies to home loans sanctioned and disbursed on or after 1 September 2024.

## Subsidy Amount

The subsidy is 4% interest on the first ₹8 lakh of the home loan, calculated over up to 12 years, with a maximum benefit of ₹1.80 lakh. The loan itself can be larger than ₹8 lakh, but the subsidy is calculated only on the first ₹8 lakh. The subsidy is released to the borrower's loan account in up to five yearly instalments.

## Who Is Eligible

Families in the Economically Weaker Section (annual household income up to ₹3 lakh), Low Income Group (₹3 lakh to ₹6 lakh) and Middle Income Group (₹6 lakh to ₹9 lakh). The family must not own a pucca house anywhere in India. The home loan must be up to ₹25 lakh, for a house worth up to ₹35 lakh, with a carpet area up to 120 square metres.

## How to Apply

Apply on the PMAY-U 2.0 portal (pmaymis.gov.in) or through a participating bank or housing finance company when taking the home loan. The loan account must stay regular for the instalments to be released.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**I earn ₹8 lakh a year and am buying my first flat for ₹30 lakh with a ₹20 lakh loan. Could I be eligible?**
Your income falls in the Middle Income Group and the loan and property values are within limits, so you may be eligible, subject to the scheme's other conditions and the lender's checks.
