# LoanMate Bank (Demo) — Prepayment Policy

> This is a fictional demo policy of "LoanMate Bank," created for the LoanMate AI academic project. It does not describe a real bank's policy.

## What Is Prepayment

Prepayment means paying an extra lump-sum amount towards your loan's outstanding principal, in addition to your regular EMIs. Prepayment reduces the outstanding balance, which in turn reduces either your future EMI amount or your remaining tenure, depending on the option you choose.

## When Prepayment Is Allowed

Prepayment is allowed on all LoanMate loan products after a minimum of 12 EMIs have been paid. This waiting period ensures the loan has been active long enough to establish a stable repayment record.

## Prepayment Charges

For floating-rate loans (the most common type at LoanMate), a foreclosure/prepayment charge of 2% to 5% of the outstanding principal may apply, depending on the loan product and the amount being prepaid. Fixed-rate loans may carry different, generally higher prepayment charges — please check your specific loan agreement.

## How Prepayment Affects Your Loan

When you make a prepayment, LoanMate recalculates your loan using your original interest rate and remaining tenure, applied to the new, lower outstanding balance. In the standard LoanMate demo calculation, the remaining tenure is kept the same and the EMI amount is reduced. (Note: some real-world lenders instead offer to keep the EMI the same and reduce the tenure — always confirm which option applies to your actual loan.)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a minimum prepayment amount?**
LoanMate does not enforce a minimum prepayment amount in this demo policy, but very small prepayments may not meaningfully change your EMI.

**Can I prepay multiple times?**
Yes, prepayment can be made more than once during the loan tenure, subject to the same 12-EMI waiting period being met at least once.
